How your floor is set
Your floor is the point where your sales cover the cost of your role. Expenses are estimated for the months not yet entered and trued to actuals in January: lower actual expenses lower your floor. Every input moves with the live numbers, so this can shift a little from night to night.
Active = 3+ orders and $2,000+ this year, or $5,000+ on dollars alone. Retained = active in 2025 and again in 2026. LCBO-routed sales count at 50¢ on the dollar toward Leg 1 and in full for the active test. Event sales sit outside Leg 1, and their margin lowers your floor instead. Figures refresh every night; "updated" above shows the last refresh. Everything settles at the January true-up.
